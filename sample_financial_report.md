## Part II — Legal Compliance, Credit Facility Covenants & Risk Disclosures

### Section 4.02 — Financial Maintenance Covenants
Under the Credit Agreement dated March 15, 2022, between Apex Global Holdings Corp. and JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., as Administrative Agent:
1. **Consolidated Leverage Ratio:** The Company covenants that it shall not permit its Consolidated Net Debt to Consolidated EBITDA Ratio to exceed **3.25 to 1.00** as of the end of any fiscal quarter. As of September 30, 2024, the Company's actual Leverage Ratio was **1.19 to 1.00**, maintaining full covenant compliance.
2. **Interest Coverage Ratio:** The Company covenants to maintain a minimum ratio of Consolidated EBITDA to Consolidated Interest Expense of at least **4.00 to 1.00**. As of September 30, 2024, the actual ratio was **15.74 to 1.00**.
3. **Negative Pledge & Restriction on Liens:** The Company and its Restricted Subsidiaries shall not incur, assume, or permit to exist any Lien upon any of their properties or assets securing indebtedness, subject to customary permitted encumbrances and a basket not to exceed **$150.0 million**.
4. **Governing Law:** This Credit Agreement and any claims, controversy, or dispute arising out of or related thereto shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the law of the **State of New York**.

### Section 5.01 — Risk Factors & Litigation Disclosures
* **Foreign Currency Exchange Exposure:** The Company generates 42% of revenue from international markets (primarily Eurozone and APAC). Currency fluctuations, particularly appreciation of the U.S. Dollar against the EUR and JPY, could materially compress operating margins.
* **Ongoing Antitrust Regulatory Inquiry:** In June 2024, the European Commission initiated a preliminary information request regarding European distribution exclusivity agreements. Management believes the allegations lack merit, and no accrual has been recorded as potential loss is neither probable nor estimable at this time.
